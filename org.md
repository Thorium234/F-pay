**Farmer Pay (F-Pay)**  
**Digital Payment & Traceability Solution for Smallholder Coffee Farmers**

### 1. Problem Statement
Small-scale coffee farmers in Kenya face persistent challenges in receiving fair, timely, and secure payments for their produce. The current system relies heavily on manual processes: farmers deliver coffee to factories or cooperatives, receive paper receipts, and later travel to collect cash payments. This model creates significant risks around underpayment, lack of transparency, delayed disbursements, and physical insecurity.

### 2. Context and Evidence
Field insights from coffee-growing areas (including Bungoma and related regions) highlight recurring issues:

- Farmers deliver coffee and receive signed paper receipts, yet payment amounts frequently do not match the recorded volumes. In one documented 2024 case, a farmer whose delivery totaled approximately 2,000 kg was paid for only about 1,000 kg, with limited practical means to challenge the discrepancy.
- Cash handling exposes both farmers and cooperative staff to crime. In 2022, a cooperative officer in Nakuru withdrew KES 2 million to pay farmers and was abducted en route; the funds were lost.
- Data from 2025 indicates that more than ten individuals were killed after collecting payments from coffee cooperatives.
- Additional systemic problems include:
  - Delayed payments (often three months or longer) with little visibility into calculations.
  - Difficulty retaining and verifying paper records over multiple seasons or years.
  - The requirement for farmers to travel to cooperatives on payday, carrying or collecting cash in environments with elevated security risks.

These issues undermine farmer trust, reduce incentives for quality production, and expose vulnerable rural households to financial loss and personal danger.

### 3. Proposed Solution: Farmer Pay (F-Pay)
F-Pay is a digital platform designed to replace manual cash-based settlement with secure, transparent, and verifiable electronic payments. It prioritizes accessibility for farmers with basic feature phones as well as smartphones.

**Core components**
- Dual-access channels: USSD (for feature phones) and a mobile application (for smartphones).
- Immutable transaction records supported by blockchain or equivalent distributed-ledger technology, ensuring every delivery, calculation, and payment is permanently timestamped and auditable.
- Automated SMS notifications for delivery confirmation, payment processing, and account updates.
- Digital collection statements and multi-year payment history accessible to the farmer at any time.
- Direct electronic disbursement to reduce or eliminate cash handling.

### 4. Expected Benefits
- **Security**: Removes the need for large cash withdrawals and farmer travel on payday, significantly reducing exposure to theft, abduction, and violence.
- **Transparency & Accountability**: Farmers can independently verify volumes delivered against amounts paid, with a permanent digital trail.
- **Timeliness**: Enables faster settlement cycles compared with the current multi-month delays.
- **Record-keeping**: Farmers retain accessible proof of earnings over seasons and years without relying on fragile paper receipts.
- **Inclusion**: USSD support ensures the solution reaches farmers who do not own smartphones.

### 5. Alignment with Broader Goals
F-Pay addresses critical pain points in the coffee value chain that affect livelihoods, trust in cooperatives, and rural safety. By leveraging existing mobile infrastructure (USSD, SMS, and apps) together with modern ledger technology, the solution is practical for the Kenyan smallholder context while offering a clear upgrade path toward greater formalization and financial inclusion.
